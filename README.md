# Pragmatic Audio Player

**Your own music, tuned to your headphones.** Pragmatic Audio Player plays music from Plex, your cloud drives and your own files,
with a full parametric EQ built in, optional effects, live views of the sound, one search across everything you add, and voice
search with Siri on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Made by [Pragmatic Audio](https://www.pragmaticaudio.com/).

This repository holds the **macOS download** (see [Releases](../../releases)). The source is not published here.

## Where to get it

- **Mac:** download the `.dmg` from [Releases](../../releases), open it and drag Pragmatic Audio Player to Applications. It is
  signed and notarised by Apple.
- **In your browser:** [informedlistening.com/pragmaticaudio-player](https://informedlistening.com/pragmaticaudio-player/).
- **iPhone and iPad, Android, Windows:** through the App Store, Google Play and the Microsoft Store, as each is approved. The links
  are on the [apps page](https://www.pragmaticaudio.com/apps/).

## Privacy

No accounts of ours, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. See the
[privacy policy](https://www.pragmaticaudio.com/privacy.html).
